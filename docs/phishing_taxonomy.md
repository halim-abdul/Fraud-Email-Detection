# Fraud and phishing taxonomy

- **Extortion:** leaked-data claims, deadlines and crypto payment demands.
- **Lottery scams:** unlikely winnings and advanced payment demands.
- **Credential phishing:** fake password resets and sign-in collection links.
- **Business email compromise:** impersonated payment instruction changes and urgent transfers.
- **Bulk spam:** non-targeted promotion; may be legal but irrelevant.
- **Legitimate relevant:** messages needing human business response.
- **Legitimate irrelevant:** benign mail to archive after review.

Categories overlap; do not conflate marketing language and fraud conviction.
